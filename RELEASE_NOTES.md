## Touhou 10 — 1.00a-r7

* 🧊 **No more freeze when you capture a spell card.** The moment a card is captured, every bullet turns into an item at once. That moment used to stall the picture down to about 10–15 FPS; it now holds about **48 FPS** at its worst — roughly **4× (+300%)** the frame rate of r6.
* ⏱️ **Slowdown like on PC:** a heavy frame now briefly slows the game instead of skipping ahead, so you never get several frames dropped at once in the middle of a pattern.
* 💾 Same folder, saves, replays and controls.

Just replace `touhou10.nro`.
